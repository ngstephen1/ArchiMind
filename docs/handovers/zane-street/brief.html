Reading the plan against the photograph

Triangle 6 points toward the north bay. The drawing labels this bedroom 11 × 14 ft (3.3528 × 4.2672 m). The projecting bay makes the room outline irregular.

GEOMETRY TO CONFIRM

Record the finished-face wall runs in sequence, two diagonals, bay angles, ceiling height and any uneven floor levels.

OPENINGS AND SERVICES

Locate the entry and swing. Measure each sash, sill, head, casing projection and operable clearance. Identify power points and heating equipment.

DESIGN QUESTION

Trial a desk on a side wall to retain views into the bay. Check screen glare at the intended seat and test chair pullback against the measured circulation route before selecting furniture.

CLIENT DECISIONS

Confirm current access, intended use, pieces to retain, budget and currency. The archive establishes the historical case. A new field survey and client brief are required to issue a proposal for the present property.
